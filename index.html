Privacy Policy for Stretch Punch

Developer: MindOops Studio
Package name: com.mindoops.stretchpunch
Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio handles information in connection with Stretch Punch, a single-player boxing puzzle game for Android.

1. Information Players Provide

Stretch Punch does not require account registration, login, chat, player profiles, or personal information forms. During normal gameplay, the game does not ask you to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment information, contacts, photos, videos, recordings, files, or user-generated content.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and provide support.

2. Local Game Data

The game stores preferences, including sound and vibration settings, locally on your device. Game data stored locally is used to support gameplay and remember your preferences. MindOops Studio does not operate player accounts or a server-side player account database for Stretch Punch.

3. Permissions

Stretch Punch uses vibration for optional haptic feedback. You can turn this off in the game settings. Standard network permissions may be included for engine or platform functionality. These permissions are not used by MindOops Studio to access your contacts, media, microphone, camera, or precise location.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Purchases

The current version does not show third-party advertisements, offer in-app purchases, or include developer-enabled gameplay analytics. MindOops Studio does not use advertising identifiers for ad targeting, ad measurement, profiling, or marketing.

5. Platform and Technical Services

Stretch Punch is built with Unity and distributed through Google Play. Google Play, Android, device manufacturers, and engine or operating-system services may process technical information as applicable to their services and your settings. This can include device and app information, identifiers, crash reports, diagnostics, and performance information used for distribution, security, compatibility, or service operation.

This does not mean that every listed provider receives every category of data from the game. Their processing and retention are governed by their own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Unity's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing

MindOops Studio does not sell player information or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Email support information may be processed by our email service provider to deliver and manage correspondence. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and security.

7. Retention and Deletion

You can remove local game data by clearing Stretch Punch's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Device backups, if enabled, are managed separately through your device or backup provider.

Support emails are retained only as long as needed to handle your request, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us below. Information independently processed by platform providers is subject to their retention policies and user controls.

8. Children's Privacy

The game does not ask children to submit personal information during gameplay. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle, including support correspondence. However, no device, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. External Links and Policy Changes

External websites are governed by their own privacy policies. We may update this policy when the game or our practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date, and additional notice will be provided where required.

11. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning information held by MindOops Studio, email mindsoops@gmail.com.
