Privacy Policy for Stretch Punch

Game: Stretch Punch: Cat Puzzle
Developer: MindOops Studio
Package name: com.mindoops.stretchpunch
Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with Stretch Punch: Cat Puzzle, including its integrated publishing, advertising, analytics, and attribution services. MindOops Studio develops the game and integrates YSO Corp GameUtils and related third-party services.

1. Information You Provide

The game does not require an online account, social login, chat, or a personal-information form. If you contact us for support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The game stores progress, completed levels, virtual gold, hint balances, tutorial progress, language, sound, music, vibration, and notification preferences locally. This information supports gameplay and remembers your choices. Device backups, where enabled, are managed through Android or your backup provider.

3. Information Processed by Integrated Services

Publishing, advertising, and measurement SDKs may collect or receive the following information, depending on the service, device settings, permissions, and applicable privacy choices:

  • Identifiers: advertising identifiers where available, app or device-related identifiers, session identifiers, and installation or attribution information.
  • Gameplay and usage: app launches, sessions, play duration, level starts and results, progression events, and interactions with game features.
  • Advertising activity: ad impressions, clicks, reward-completion events, ad placement and network information, and advertising revenue or measurement data.
  • Technical and network information: device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, memory and processor information, performance information, IP address, connection information, language, country, and approximate location derived from information such as an IP address.

These identifiers and events can be associated with a device or usage history even when no online account exists. Not every provider receives every category. SDK-related processing is not limited to occasions when you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate and improve the game, understand level difficulty and performance, diagnose technical problems, measure installations and advertising campaigns, deliver and measure advertisements, validate ad rewards, and help detect fraud or misuse. Advertising partners may use information for personalized advertising depending on applicable law, their services, and your privacy choices.

Rewarded advertisements are optional offers for in-game benefits such as gold, hints, or a continue. The current game does not offer real-money in-app purchases. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad is separate from the privacy choices available for integrated SDKs.

5. Service Providers and Their Policies

  • YSO Corp / GameUtils: publishing tools, gameplay analytics, configuration, and advertising-related services. YSO Corp Privacy Policy.
  • AppLovin MAX: advertising mediation and advertising-related measurement. AppLovin Privacy Policy and partner information.
  • Tenjin: installation attribution and campaign measurement. Tenjin Privacy Policy.
  • Meta / Facebook SDK: app and installation event measurement and attribution. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Unity and Google: game-engine, advertising-network where used, platform, distribution, and related technical services. Unity Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Additional advertising partners may participate through the integrated mediation services. Their involvement depends on configuration and ad delivery. The YSO Corp and AppLovin policies provide further information about participating partners, their processing, and available choices.

6. Privacy Choices and Permissions

Where a consent message is presented, use it to make the available choices about optional advertising purposes and partners. Available privacy controls can be accessed through the game's Settings > Data Privacy section. Their availability depends on your region and the advertising service's configuration. Reading this policy or continuing to play does not, by itself, constitute consent to personalized advertising.

Android provides additional advertising-identifier and privacy controls, which vary by device and Android version. You can disable optional game reminders through the game's notification settings or Android settings, and vibration through the game's haptics setting. Network access supports the integrated services. Core gameplay does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or GPS location.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent through available controls or contact us for assistance. Withdrawal does not undo processing already carried out lawfully. Contact us if a privacy control is unavailable or you need help exercising a choice.

7. Sharing and International Processing

Relevant information may be shared with the publishing, analytics, attribution, advertising, hosting, and support providers involved in operating the game. Advertising-related disclosures may qualify as sharing or targeted advertising under some privacy laws. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

Providers may process information outside your country. Their policies explain their processing locations, international transfers, safeguards, and responsibilities. Contact us with questions about information associated with this game.

8. Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

Local game data remains on your device until removed or changed through gameplay, app settings, clearing app storage, or uninstalling. Support correspondence is retained as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. SDK providers apply their own retention arrangements as described in their policies; there is no single retention period covering every provider.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not automatically delete information already received by third-party services. Contact us for assistance with a deletion request, or use the relevant provider's privacy-request process. We may need limited information to identify the relevant records and verify your request.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of applicable advertising-related sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. Contact us to request assistance. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

The game does not provide forms requesting children's names, contact details, or other personal information. Integrated advertising and measurement services can nevertheless process device and usage information as described above. If you believe a child's information has been processed without any required parental permission, contact us so we can investigate and coordinate appropriate action with the relevant providers, including deletion where applicable.

10. Security and External Links

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Advertisements and other external links may open third-party websites or services whose own privacy policies apply to their activities.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game, integrated services, or our information practices change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. Additional notices or renewed consent will be provided where required.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning Stretch Punch: Cat Puzzle, contact MindOops Studio at mindsoops@gmail.com. For information handled by YSO Corp, you can also consult the contact and rights information in its Privacy Policy.
