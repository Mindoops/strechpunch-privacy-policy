Privacy Policy

Game: Ninja Cat: Slice & Match
Developer: MindOops Studio
Android package name: com.mindoops.stretchpunch
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindOops Studio and the services integrated into Ninja Cat: Slice & Match handle information when you play the game or contact us for support. The game integrates YSO Corp GameUtils and third-party services for publishing, advertising, analytics, and installation attribution.

1. Information You Provide

The game does not require an online account or social login and does not include chat or a personal-information form. If you email us for support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to and follow up on your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The game saves your level progress, unlocked features, virtual coin balance, tutorial progress, and preferences for language, sound, music, vibration, and reminders on your device.

Integrated services may also store identifiers, configuration, privacy choices, and advertising or measurement information on your device. If device backup is enabled, Android or your backup provider manages that backup.

3. Information Processed by Integrated Services

Publishing, advertising, and measurement services may collect or receive the following information. The information processed depends on the service, its configuration, your device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

  • Identifiers and attribution: advertising identifiers where available, app or device-related identifiers, session identifiers, and installation or campaign attribution information.
  • Gameplay and usage: app launches, sessions, play duration, level starts and results, progress, and interactions with game features.
  • Advertising activity: ad impressions, clicks, video completions, reward events, ad placements, network information, and advertising revenue or measurement data.
  • Technical and network information: device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, screen and performance information, IP address, connection information, language, country, and approximate location inferred from information such as an IP address.
  • Privacy signals: applicable consent choices, advertising preferences, and device-level tracking or advertising settings.

Identifiers and events may be associated with a device or its usage history even when you play without an account. The categories vary by provider. Some services process information during app use independently of whether you choose to watch a rewarded ad.

4. How Information Is Used

We and our service providers use information to operate and improve the game, understand level difficulty and performance, diagnose technical problems, measure installations and campaigns, deliver and measure ads, validate ad rewards, remember privacy choices, and detect fraud or misuse. Advertising partners may use information to personalize ads where permitted by applicable law and your privacy choices.

You can choose to watch rewarded ads for virtual coins or extra slashes. The Shop offers coins as an ad reward, and coins can be used to get extra slashes when offered. The current game does not offer purchases with real money. Virtual coins have no cash value. Rewarded ad choices and advertising privacy controls are managed separately.

5. Service Providers and Their Policies

  • YSO Corp / GameUtils: publishing tools, gameplay analytics, configuration, and advertising-related services. YSO Corp Privacy Policy.
  • AppLovin MAX: advertising mediation and advertising measurement. AppLovin Privacy Policy and AppLovin partner privacy information.
  • Tenjin: installation attribution and campaign measurement. Tenjin Privacy Policy.
  • Meta / Facebook SDK: app and installation event measurement and attribution. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Voodoo ADN / Helix: advertising signals, measurement, and advertising services where enabled. Helix Privacy Policy.
  • Unity and Google: game engine, advertising where used, platform, distribution, and related technical services. Unity Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Other advertising partners may participate through the integrated mediation services depending on configuration and ad delivery. The provider policies describe how these services process information and what privacy choices they offer.

6. Privacy Choices, Permissions, and Reminders

If the game presents a consent message, you can use it to make the available choices about advertising purposes and partners. To review privacy options, open Home > Settings > Data Privacy. The controls shown depend on your region and the advertising service's configuration. Reading this policy or continuing to play does not by itself constitute consent to personalized advertising.

Android provides additional advertising identifier and privacy controls that vary by device and Android version. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it through the available controls or contact us for assistance. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew consent.

If you enable reminders and grant any required notification permission, the game schedules up to two local reminders per day using your device's local time and the game's selected language. You can turn reminders off in Home Settings or Android's notification settings.

You can change sound, music, and vibration preferences in the game. Network access supports integrated services. Core gameplay does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

7. Sharing and International Processing

We may share relevant information with the publishing, analytics, attribution, advertising, hosting, and support providers involved in operating the game. Advertising disclosures may qualify as sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under some privacy laws. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

Providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their policies provide details about international processing, applicable safeguards, and their responsibilities.

8. Retention, Deletion, and Your Rights

Local game data stays on your device until it is changed through gameplay or settings, or removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling. We retain support correspondence as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. Integrated providers apply their own retention arrangements as described in their policies.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not automatically delete information already received by third-party services. Contact us for help with a deletion request or use the relevant provider's privacy request process. We may need limited information to identify the records and verify your request.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of applicable advertising-related sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Use the contact details below to request assistance.

9. Children's Privacy

The game does not include forms asking children to provide names, contact details, or other personal information. Integrated advertising and measurement services may process device and usage information as described in this policy. If you believe a child's information was processed without required parental permission, contact us so we can investigate and coordinate with the relevant providers, including on deletion where applicable.

10. Security and External Links

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security. Ads and other external links may take you to third-party websites or services. Their privacy policies govern their activities.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the game, its integrated services, or our information practices change. We will post the updated policy here with a revised date and provide additional notice or seek renewed consent where required.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about Ninja Cat: Slice & Match, contact MindOops Studio at mindsoops@gmail.com. For information handled by YSO Corp, see the contact and rights information in the YSO Corp Privacy Policy.
